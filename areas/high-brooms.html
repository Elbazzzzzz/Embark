Dog Walking in High Brooms

Group dog walks for High Brooms, the northern neighbourhood near the station, with convenient pick-up and woodland routes beyond the residential streets.

High Brooms sits to the north of Royal Tunbridge Wells, a practical, well-connected neighbourhood anchored by its own railway station and a mix of Victorian terraces and family homes. Many residents here commute by train and need a dog walker who can collect from home while they are at work, particularly around the Grosvenor Road and Hilbert Road area, where I pick up regularly.

Embark collects dogs from across High Brooms on weekday slots around 9am, 11am and 2pm. From this northern edge of town, my walks head north and north-west into woodland beyond the residential streets, forest tracks and shaded paths where dogs get at least an hour of off-lead exercise in carefully selected, limited groups. It is a world away from pacing the same few streets near the station, and it gives working owners genuine peace of mind on commute days.

The routine is simple: I arrive at your door, greet your dog, drive to one of my woodland locations, walk, then bring them home wiped down and settled. High Brooms dogs often join the same walking pack each week, building steady friendships with their groupmates. That socialisation is a core part of what I do, not just miles covered, but calm, positive interactions with other dogs under experienced supervision.

If you live in High Brooms and need a reliable local dog walker, I would be glad to help. See all the neighbourhoods I cover on my areas page, read about pricing on services and prices, or get in touch to book a free consultation and confirm your postcode.
