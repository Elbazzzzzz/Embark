Areas We Cover

We operate within a 3 mile radius of TN1, Royal Tunbridge Wells, picking up dogs from town and the surrounding villages for woodland group walks.

Our Service Area

Embark is based in Royal Tunbridge Wells and covers the town centre plus the villages and neighbourhoods within roughly three miles. That means regular pick-ups from Southborough, Rusthall, Langton Green, Hawkenbury, Pembury, High Brooms and plenty of TN postcodes in between.

Not sure if you're within range? Get in touch with your postcode and we'll let you know straight away. If you're in, we'll arrange a free consultation to meet you and your dog.

Areas We Serve

Southborough

Professional dog walking for Southborough residents, with pick-up and woodland group walks heading into the High Weald.

Rusthall

Dog walking near Rusthall and Tunbridge Wells Common, perfect for dogs who love woodland adventures.

Langton Green

Reliable group walks for Langton Green, with easy access to forest routes west of town.

Hawkenbury

Pick-up and drop-off dog walking for Hawkenbury, with your dog home clean and worn out.

Pembury

Dog walking for Pembury village, with woodland walks east of Royal Tunbridge Wells.

High Brooms

Group dog walks for High Brooms, with convenient pick-up from this northern neighbourhood.
