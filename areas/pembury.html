Dog Walking in Pembury

Dog walking for Pembury village, pick-up along the A264 corridor and woodland group walks heading east of Royal Tunbridge Wells.

Pembury is a proper village in its own right, strung along the A264 east of Royal Tunbridge Wells with a mix of period cottages, newer estates and the kind of community feel that town-centre postcodes cannot quite replicate. Dogs are everywhere, in gardens along the High Street, on the fields near the village edge, and on weekend strolls through the surrounding countryside. But weekday walks are another matter, especially if you are commuting towards Tunbridge Wells or London and your dog is left with a fraction of the exercise they really need.

Embark collects dogs from Pembury on weekday slots around 9am, 11am and 2pm, picking up from homes across the village and the connecting residential roads off the A264. From Pembury, our walks head east into the woodland routes that lie beyond the village, shaded tracks, open rides and forest loops that give dogs a full hour of off-lead exploration. Each group is carefully selected and limited so every member of the pack gets a calm, enjoyable walk and all dogs stay safe.

The service is fully managed: pick-up, woodland walk, drop-off and a wiped-down dog at the end. Pembury owners appreciate not having to drive into town for a walker, or rely on a neighbour when plans change. We are local, insured, and have been walking dogs in this area since 2017.
