How It Works

  1. Pick-up from your door

    I collect your dog from home within my 3 mile radius of TN1. No hassle for you, just a friendly face at the door.

  2. 1 hour+ woodland group walk

    Group walks are limited to a maximum of six dogs. Every dog has a trial period to ensure they're the right fit for an Embark walk. Hand picked groups mean safer walks, managed in a responsible way in an appropriate environment. Dogs who are well socialised with matched temperaments and good recall create a happy walk with no stress. How every dog walk should be!

  3. Drop-off: Happy and worn out

    Your dog comes home tired, happy and ready for a nap. I wipe muddy paws and send you updates along the way.

Life on an Embark walk

Muddy paws, woodland paths, and a happy pack — moments from my Tunbridge Wells group walks.

More Than Just a Walk

All dogs need more than just physical exercise. They also have emotional, mental and behavioural needs.Embark prioritises these factors in the way I walk, handle and care for your dog to ensure your dog is exercised, enriched and socialised.

Whether you need a regular weekly slot or an ad-hoc walk while you're at work, I'm here to help. Learn more on my services page, read what clients say in my reviews, or browse the FAQ if you have questions.
