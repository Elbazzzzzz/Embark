About Embark

A holistic dog walking service based in Royal Tunbridge Wells and surrounding areas, run with care, experience and genuine enthusiasm for dogs.

Meet Joe

Hello! I'm Joe and I own Embark. I am eager, experienced and have the passion and enthusiasm that is required as a dog walker. In 2015 I completed a two-year full time extended diploma animal management course, covering areas ranging from behaviour to welfare, and have over a year's experience volunteering at Battersea Dogs Home. All have provided me with the skills, knowledge and the positive approach necessary to ensure that your dog is in the best possible hands. I have owned Embark Dog Walking since 2017.

The Team

Embark is built on personal, hands-on care. Joe leads every walk and manages client relationships day to day. As I grow, I'm building a small team of walkers who share the same standards: insured, pet first aid qualified, and genuinely mad about dogs.

More walkers coming soon
