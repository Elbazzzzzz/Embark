Group Dog Walking in Tunbridge Wells

Holistic woodland group walks with pick-up and drop-off, because your dog deserves more than a quick lap around the block.

What a Walk Looks Like

Every Embark walk follows the same thoughtful rhythm, whether it's your dog's first time with me or their hundredth. It starts at your door: I arrive in the Embark van, greet your dog, and help them settle in alongside their walking companions for the day.

I drive to one of my local woodland or forest locations, places where dogs can sniff, explore and run off lead in a safe, supervised group. Walks last a minimum of one hour, often longer depending on the group and the weather. There's time for play, scent work, calm moments and plenty of socialisation with other steady, friendly dogs.

When I'm done, I drop your dog home. They come back clean and worn out, muddy paws wiped, water topped up, and usually ready for a long nap. You'll hear from me with updates about how the walk went. That's the Embark difference: exercise, enrichment and socialisation, all in one trip.

Daily Walk Slots

I run three group walk slots every weekday: approximately 9am, 11am and 2pm. Most regular clients book the same slot each week so their dog gets to know their walking pack. Ad-hoc walks are available subject to space, so just get in touch and I'll see what I can do.

Small Groups, Big Adventures

Each group is carefully selected and limited to ensure the best possible walk, and the safety of all dogs. That means matching temperaments thoughtfully, keeping numbers sensible, and making sure nobody gets overwhelmed. Your dog gets the benefits of socialisation without the chaos of a large pack.

Pick-up and Drop-off

I collect and return your dog within a 3 mile radius of TN1, Royal Tunbridge Wells. That covers Southborough, Rusthall, Langton Green, Hawkenbury, Pembury, High Brooms and surrounding TN postcodes. See my areas page for more detail on where I pick up.

My Holistic Approach

All dogs need more than just physical exercise. They also have emotional, mental and behavioural needs.Embark prioritises these factors in the way I walk, handle and care for your dog to ensure your dog is exercised, enriched and socialised. It's why I choose woodlands over pavements, small groups over crowds, and calm leadership over rushing.

Requirements

To keep every dog safe and happy in the group, I ask that:

  • Dogs over 1 year old are neutered
  • All dogs are well socialised and comfortable around other dogs
  • All dogs can walk off lead with reliable recall
